# Dragonfall lore bible

**Status:** author-supplied (2026-08-14)  
**Tagline:** *From the plunge, a new age is born.*  
**Product screen:** Dragonfall

---

## 1. Cosmology — The Plunge

In the age before ages, a colossal dragon known as the **Sky-Breaker** (or the **First Wyrm**) ruled the high firmament. Its body was the living axis of the world: scales of living stone, blood of molten star-metal, breath that shaped weather and seasons.

When the dragon grew weary of eternity—or was wounded by a forgotten rival, or chose sacrifice—it folded its wings and **fell**.

The impact carved a wound so deep it split continents, oceans, and the crust of the world. That wound is the **Scar**. From the dragon’s corpse—blood, bone, and dying fire—a new age was born:

- Mountains rose from shattered scales  
- Rivers of quicksilver and mana flowed from ruptured arteries  
- Forests of crystalline trees and living stone grew from marrow  
- The sky still carries faint auroras of the dragon’s last breath  

The tagline is literal: **from the plunge, a new age is born.** Every culture, ruin, and magic system traces back to some fragment of that fall.

---

## 2. Tone

- **Hope in the Scar** (safest, most developed place; home base, downtime, research).  
- **Wonder and hazard in the Wound-lands** (beauty grown from corpse and impact).  
- **Mythic weight late game** (unfinished purpose of the First Wyrm; rewrite or prevent a second fall).  

**Forbidden as setting identity:** Midgaard/Diku pastiche, Keep on the Borderlands module names, Vecna-as-Vecna, generic D&D giant steading labels as the “true” world. Legacy filenames may remain on disk until renamed; player-facing strings should be Dragonfall.

---

## 3. Geography — The Scar

The **Scar** is both a geographic feature and a living settlement built into and around the dragon’s final resting place.

- Central town occupies the deepest stable section of the impact trench: a long, irregular canyon of fused dragonbone and impact-glass.  
- Homes, workshops, and the central **Keep** are carved into rib-cage and vertebrae. The **Great Spine** is the main thoroughfare.  
- A permanent low-level **aurora** hangs over the Scar at night (residual dragon-fire). Flora and fauna are subtly dragon-touched.  
- The town is the safest, most developed place in the known world. Adventurers leave to explore the wider **Wound-lands** and return to sell, rest, research, and advance.  
- The dragon’s **heart still faintly beats** here—a deep pulse in the stone. Residual life-force keeps the town stable and powers local **dragon-touched** magic that characters begin with.

**Capital role (was Midgaard):** The Scar settlement.  
**Temple / recall (vnum 3001):** Heart-Pulse sanctum / place of return along the Great Spine.  
**School (vnum 3700):** First awakening of Scar-born magic / Scale-touched training grounds.

### Surrounding structure

Progression is **ascent of the wound** (geographic + metaphysical):

| Band | Meaning | Player fantasy |
|------|---------|----------------|

## 4. What the character is doing

You are **Scar-born** (or a newcomer drawn to the Scar). Residual dragon-magic has begun to awaken fragments of the First Wyrm’s memory, power, or unfinished purpose.

**Overarching goal:** ascend the wound—climb, delve, and traverse layered remnants of the dragon’s body and the lands its fall reshaped—gathering scattered essence (**scales, blood, bone, breath, will**) so that you can either:

1. **Finish** what the dragon started,  
2. **Prevent** a second fall, or  
3. **Become** the next axis of the world.

Reaching the top means standing where residual god-like authority still holds—where the new age can be rewritten.

## 6. Faiths / powers

- The **First Wyrm** is not a temple god in the Diku sense; it is the **axis that fell**.  
- Public devotion in the Scar centers on **gratitude to residual life** (Heart-Pulse) and **stewardship of the new age**.  
- Taboo: attempts to **force a second plunge**, desecrate the Heart-Pulse, or sell living marrow outside regulated Scalewright channels.

## 11. Style notes for room prose

- Prefer concrete sensory detail: bone-glass underfoot, aurora on the canyon rim, the slow heart-pulse.  
- One beat of myth per room max; avoid repeating the full Plunge story every room.  
- Color codes: keep existing `@@…@@N` conventions; gold/amber for residual fire, cyan for aurora, dark for deep wound.  
- Avoid “This place tests travelers of levels X–Y” as the main description line.
